## Mac & Mac Store storefront consolidation

This draft implements the approved storefront evolution as isolated, reusable components while keeping `main` untouched.

### Included
- Central store + WhatsApp configuration
- Promotional carousel data contract
- Reusable product media/card/grid foundation
- Mac Advisor entry point
- Accessories, benefits, technical service, customer care
- Testimonials, social community, newsletter
- Complete legal footer with SIC destination
- Responsive styling and implementation/release safeguards

### Not yet integrated
The current `App.jsx` remains unchanged. Validation and replacement of the legacy lower sections are the next phase.

### Do not merge yet
Requires lint/build, desktop/mobile visual review, verified social/legal/location data, and explicit approval.
